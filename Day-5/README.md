# Day 5 – SOQL, DML and Apex Business Logic

## Objective

The objective of Day 5 was to implement business logic for the Airport Ground Operations Management System using Apex, SOQL, and DML.

The focus was on retrieving flight and ground service information, applying business rules, and updating Salesforce records based on operational requirements.

## Work Completed

- Used SOQL to retrieve Flight and Ground Service Task records.
- Implemented Apex classes to handle business logic.
- Used DML operations to create and update Salesforce records.
- Implemented business validations before modifying records.
- Added logic to process ground service tasks related to flights.
- Updated Flight records based on the completion of required ground services.
- Followed separation of responsibilities between Apex classes and handlers.

## Key Concepts

- SOQL
- DML
- Apex Classes
- Business Logic
- Record Retrieval
- Record Creation and Updates
- Data Validation
- Service Layer

## Learning Outcome

Learned how to combine SOQL, DML, and Apex business logic to retrieve Salesforce data, apply business rules, and safely update records.

## Conclusion

Day 5 strengthened the backend of the Airport Ground Operations Management System by implementing Apex logic that connects Salesforce data with real-world airport operational requirements.
